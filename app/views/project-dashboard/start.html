Use this service to upload GeoPackage files (.gpkg) and check your Biodiversity Net Gain (BNG) metric for baseline and post intervention.

This service is only available for:

  • Very Low, Low and Medium Distinctiveness habitats
  • on-site Biodiversity Net Gain

You can use this service to edit your information to meet your Biodiversity Net Gain target. You can also check for any flags and ensure you have met trading rules.

What you need

To use the Biodiversity Net Gain metric you will need:

  • the name of your project and project details
  • a Biodiversity Net Gain account using DefraID
  • a baseline Biodiversity Net Gain GeoPackage (.gpkg) file
  • a post-intervention Biodiversity Net Gain GeoPackage (.gpkg) file
